# BluePeak Corporate Commute Regulations

## 1. Scope of Transport Operations
BluePeak Technologies provides shared smart-shuttle and private cab transport for all permanent staff members whose primary workplace is the BluePeak Technology Center. Transport covers designated metropolitan radial routes up to 35 km.

## 2. Night Cab Security and Escort
BluePeak mandates a certified security marshal for women employees on any night shift drop scheduled after 22:00. The security marshal is deployed directly inside the vehicle and confirms drop-off completion via digital sign-off.

## 3. Weekend Cab Operations
Commute facilities on Saturday and Sunday are not offered under corporate transport arrangements. Staff working weekend rosters must claim reimbursement through departmental cost centers.

## 4. Airport Transit Approvals
All bookings for airport drop or pickup rides require joint authorization from both HR + finance departments prior to reservation dispatch. Solo approvals from line managers are invalid for airport rides.

## 5. No-Show Violations and Levies
Employees who fail to board without cancelling at least 90 minutes in advance receive an automated warning for first two; ₹300 from third unexcused no-show in a calendar quarter, deducted directly via payroll adjustments.

## 6. Daily Booking Cut-Off
The booking cut-off for all morning and evening rostered rides is strictly 2 hours before pickup. Late requests will be held in waitlist status and are not guaranteed confirmation.

## 7. Vehicle Conduct and Luggage Constraints
Employees are permitted one standard laptop bag and one carry-on cabin bag. Personal bulky equipment, bicycles, and hazardous cargo are forbidden on corporate vehicles.

## 8. Visitor Accompaniment
BluePeak vehicles are dedicated solely to registered BluePeak associates. Family members, contractors without transit passes, and outside visitors are not permitted under any condition.
