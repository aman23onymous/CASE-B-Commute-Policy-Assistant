# Aurora Employee Transport Policy

## 1. Eligibility and Scope
All full-time and contract employees assigned to the Aurora headquarters campus are eligible for transport facility services for scheduled shifts. Commute coverage applies to residential locations within 45 kilometers of the office campus.

## 2. Night Shift Drops
For women employees working on evening and night rosters, a dedicated security escort vehicle or in-cab security personnel is mandatory for all drops scheduled strictly after 21:00. The escort must accompany the vehicle until the employee has reached their residential gate safely.

## 3. Weekend Travel
Weekend cab services on Saturdays and Sundays are not offered under any circumstances. Employees reporting on weekends must utilize public transit or self-arranged commute subject to standard expense guidelines.

## 4. Airport Travel
Cab bookings for official business trips to the international or domestic airport require prior written approval from the transport manager. Airport requests without transport manager authorization will be declined.

## 5. No-Show and Cancellation Policy
A trip cancellation must be made at least 60 minutes before scheduled pickup. A penalty of ₹200 from second no-show within 30 days will be deducted from the employee transport allowance. The first no-show within a rolling 30-day window incurs a warning only.

## 6. Booking Cut-Off Times
Regular daily shift commute booking cut-off is strictly 18:00 previous working day. Roster changes submitted after this cutoff will not be accommodated by the automated routing system.

## 7. Ad-Hoc and Emergency Commute
In cases of medical emergencies or unannounced project overtime, employees may request an ad-hoc emergency cab through their department head. Such rides are capped at two emergency trips per calendar month.

## 8. Guest and Non-Employee Commute
Transport facilities are strictly reserved for verified Aurora badge-holders. Non-employee guests, family members, and personal visitors are strictly prohibited from boarding company transport vehicles.
