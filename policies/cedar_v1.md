# Cedar Logistics Employee Commute Guidelines

## 1. Eligibility and Commute Zones
Cedar Logistics provides operational commute services for warehouse, operations, and headquarters staff. Service operates within fixed geographic corridors extending up to 50 km from the central distribution hub.

## 2. Night Drops and Escort Guidelines
For evening and night operations, female employees traveling on shifts ending after 21:00 are accompanied by an assigned company escort guard. Escort guards ensure passenger arrival before authorizing driver return.

## 3. Weekend Commute Facility
Weekend transport on Saturdays and Sundays is available for rostered shifts only. Employees on official operational rosters published by operations management are automatically scheduled for weekend shuttles.

## 4. Airport Travel Eligibility
Airport transit is not covered under the employee commute policy. All airport journeys for business travel must be booked independently via the corporate travel portal.

## 5. No-Show Fines
To minimize fleet delays, an administrative fee of ₹100 for every no-show is levied on the offending employee account starting from the very first missed trip.

## 6. Ride Reservation Deadlines
Commute booking cut-off time is 12:00 previous calendar day for both weekday and weekend rosters. The scheduling platform locks automatically at noon.

## 7. Cargo and Personal Belongings
Passengers may carry standard work bags. Heavy warehouse supplies, freight packages, and unsanctioned parcel handling are strictly forbidden in transport vans.

## 8. Non-Employee Access
Transit vehicles are strictly restricted to Cedar Logistics employees. Guests, dependents, and third-party contractors without logistics badges are prohibited from boarding.
